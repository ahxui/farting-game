⌨️ Controls & Shortcuts
Tap the cheeks or hold Space: let it rip
Hold ↓: controlled fart (the longer you hold, the harder)
P or ↑: pump
Burp lever (next to the gas lever): pull up to swallow air, pull down to burp it out
B: quick burp
L or the 🔒 button under the gas lever: lock it open so the gas keeps discharging. Tap again (or drag the lever and let go) to unlock.
M: mute / unmute
N: open / close the behavior node editor
Twin sticks: spin the left circle (gas) and right circle (burp) with your thumbs, or both at once for a bonus. Push to the outer edge and hold to let it out.
Circle scribble: draw circles with a finger or the mouse. Left half of the screen builds gas, right half builds burp. Hold still to let it out.
Key mash: alternate ←/→ (or A/D) for gas, W/S for burp. Space lets gas out, hold Shift to burp it out.
Controller: rotate both sticks to build up (works in every style). Right trigger lets gas out, left trigger burps.
Hand tool: pick Squeeze, Slap or Cheeks in Essentials, then use your cursor on the fella
Esc: close settings
Tap the belly: poke it
🧩 Behavior Nodes Drag from a round dot on the right of a node to the dot on the left of another. Tap a wire to delete it.
